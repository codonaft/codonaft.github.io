Privacy
Not all Nostr chat clients currently support file encryption. Armada supports it, but, for instance, YakiHonne currently doesn't: if you send a file in a private chat using YakiHonne—it will be available unencrypted to everyone unrelated to your chat, who has a link to an uploaded file (a media file server owner in particular).

For paranoid nerds:


Q: Desktop/Laptop setup only?

A: Only if you know what you are doing. Possible, for instance, with nak bunker, running on a hardened enough OS. I don't recommend storing your nsec/ncryptsec directly in NIP-07 browser extensions (like nos2x) because currently every browser is a critical vulnerabilities honeypot fractal.



Q: I'm scared to carry my private key on my phone and my mobile client doesn't support Bunker URI.

A: You can connect Amber to an external bunker (it's slow but works for me) or try FROST (I didn't test it).



Q: Armada/Concord vs White Noise/Marmot vs Cordn?

A: I wish somebody could independently investigate and contribute it here. Don't take my word; verify. At least this could be outdated already.

In short:

- Armada/Concord is reliable, non-secret, and decentralized (it will load all chats from relays after logging in from a new device and decrypt them)

- White Noise is experimental, secret, and decentralized (it won't load and decrypt old chats on a new device, because relays don't store chat data after transmission)

- Cordn is a reliable, secret*, with centralized coordination (will possibly be improved with automatic coordinator leader selection in the future, but will still rely on the center(s) reliability; you can run your own center on your phone), supports multi-device sync.

All of them (even KeyChat, AFAIK) leak metadata (who/whom wrote when, but not what exactly was written) to relay operators. Avoid using your real IP if you can.
